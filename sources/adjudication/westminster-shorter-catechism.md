# THE SHORTER CATECHISM, First agreed upon by the Assembly of Divines at WESTMINSTER, And now approved by the General ASSEMBLY of the Kirk of Scotland to be a part of Uniformity in Religion between the Kirks of Christ in the three Kingdoms.

## WSC 1

*Q. 1. WHat is the chief end of man?*

A. Mans chief end is to glorifie God [marg.: 1 Cor. 10 31. Rom. 11. 36], and to enjoy him for ever [marg.: Psal 73 24, to the end].

## WSC 2

*Q. 2. What rule hath God given to direct us how we may glorifie and enjoy him?*

A. The Word of God (which is contained in the Scripture of the Old and New Testament [marg.: 2 Tim. 3 16. Eph. 2 20]) is the onely rule to direct us how we may glorifie and enjoy him [marg.: 1 Ioh. 1 3, 4].

## WSC 3

*Q. 3. VVhat do the Scriptures principally teach?*

A. The Scriptures principally teach, What man is to beleeve concerning God, and what duty God requires of man [marg.: 2 Tim. 1 13. & 3. 16].

## WSC 4

*Q. 4. VVhat is God?*

A. God is a Spirit [marg.: Joh. 4. 24], infinite [marg.: Job 11 7 8, 9], eternal [marg.: Psa. 90. 2], and unchangeable [marg.: Jam. 1. 17], in his being [marg.: Exo 3. 14], wisdom [marg.: Psa. 147. 5], power [marg.: Rev. 4. 8], holiness [marg.: Rev. 15. 4], justice, goodness, and truth [marg.: Exod. 34 6, 7].

## WSC 5

*Q. 5. Are there more Gods then one?*

A. There is but one onely, the living and true God [marg.: Deu. 6. 4 Jer. 10. 10].

## WSC 6

*Q. 6. How many Persons are there in the God-head?*

A. There are three Persons in the God-head, the Father, the Son, and the holy Ghost; and these three are one God, the same in substance, equal in power and glory [marg.: 1 Joh. 5. 7 Mat. 28. 19].

## WSC 7

*Q. 7. What are the decrees of God?*

A. The Decrees of God are, his eternal purpose according to the counsel of his Will, whereby for his own glory, he hath fore-ordained whatsoever comes to pass [marg.: Eph. 1. 4 11. Rom. 9. 22, 23].

## WSC 8

*Q. 8. How doth God execute his decrees?*

A. God executeth his Decrees in the Works of Creation and Providence:

## WSC 9

*Q. 9. What is the Work of Creation?*

A. The Work of Creation is Gods making all things of nothing, by the Word of his Power, in the space of six days, and all very good [marg.: Gen. 1. Chap: Heb. 11. 3].

## WSC 10

*Q. 10. How did God create man?*

A. God created man, male and female, after his own image, in knowledg, righteousness, and holiness, with dominion over the creatures [marg.: Gen. 1 26, 27, 28 Col. 3 10 Eph. 4 24].

## WSC 11

*Q. 11. What are Gods Works of Providence?*

A. Gods Works of Providence are his most holy [marg.: Ps. 145. 17], wise [marg.: Psa. 104 24. Isa. 28 29], and powerful preserving [marg.: Heb. 1. 3], and governing all his creatures, and all their actions [marg.: Ps. 103. 19 Mar. 10. 29 30, 31].

## WSC 12

*Q. 12. What special act of providence did God exercise toward man in the estate wherein be was created?*

A. When God had created Man, he entred into a Covenant of life with him, upon condition of perfect obedience; forbidding him to eat of the tree of knowledg of good and evil, upon pain of death [marg.: Gal. 3. 12 Gen. 2. 17].

## WSC 13

*Q. 13. Did our first Parents continue in the estate wherein they were created?*

A. Our first Parents, being left to the freedom of their own will, fell from the estate wherein they were created, by sinning against God [marg.: Gen. 3. 6 7, 8, 13 Eccl. 7 29].

## WSC 14

*Q. 14. What is sin?*

A. Sin is any want of conformity unto or transgression of the Law of God h. [marg.: 1 Ioh. 3. 4]

## WSC 15

*Q. 15. What was the sin whereby our first Parents fell from the estate wherein they were created?*

A. The sin whereby our first Parents fell from the estate wherein they were created, was their eating the forbidden fruit [marg.: Gen 3. 6 12].

## WSC 16

*Q. 16. Did all mankinde fall in Adams first transgression?*

A. The Covenant being made with Adam, not onely for himself, but for his posterity, all mankind descending from him by ordinary generation sinned in him, and fell with him in his first transgres•ion [marg.: Gen. 2. 16, 17 Rom 5 12 • Cor. 15 21, 22].

## WSC 17

*Q. 17. Into what estate did the fall bring mankinde?*

A. The fall brought mankinde into an estate of sin and misery [marg.: Rom. 5 12].

## WSC 18

*Q. 18. Wherein consists the sinfulness of that estate whereinto Man fell?*

A. The sinfulness of that estate whereinto man fell, consists in the guilt of Adams first sin, the want of O•iginal righteousness, and the corruption of his whole nature, which is commonly called Or•ginal sin, together with all actual transgressions which proceed from it [marg.: Rom. 5. 12, 19 Rom. 5 10 •• 2• Eph 2 1, 2, 3 •am. 1. 14 ••. Mat. 15 19].

## WSC 19

*Q. 19. What is the misery of that estate whereinto Man fell?*

A. All mankinde by their fall lost communion with God [marg.: Gen. 3. 8, 10, 24], are und•r his wrath and curse [marg.: Eph. 2. 2 3. gal. 3. 10], and so, made liable to all miseries in this life, to death it self, and to the pains of hell for ever [marg.: Lam 3. 39. Rom. 6. 29. Mat. 25. 41, 46].

## WSC 20

*Q. 20. Did God leave all Mankinde to perish in the estate of sin and misery?*

A. God, having out of his meer good pleasure from all eternity elected some to everlasting life [marg.: Eph. 1 4], did enter into a Covenant of grace to deliver them out of the estate of sin and misery, and to bring them into an estate of Salvation by a Redeemer [marg.: Rom. 3 20, 21, 22 Gal. 3. 21 22].

## WSC 21

*Q. 21. Who is the Redeemer of Gods elect?*

A. The only Redeemer of Gods elect, is the Lord Iesus Christ [marg.: 1 Tim 2 5, 6], who being the eternal Son of God, became man [marg.: Ioh. 1 14 Gal. 4. 4], and so, was and continueth to be God and man, in two distinct Natures, and one Person, for ever [marg.: Rom. 9. 5 Luke 1. 35 Col. 2 9. Heb. 7. 24 25].

## WSC 22

*Q. 22. How did Christ, being the Son of God, become Man?*

A. Christ, the Son of God, became man by taking to himself a true body [marg.: Heb. 2. 14, 16. and 10. 5], and a reasonable soul [marg.: Mat 26 38], being conceived by the power of the Holy Ghost in the womb of the Vi•gin Mary, and born of her [marg.: Luk. 1. 27 31, 35, 42 Gal. 4 4], yet without sin [marg.: Heb. 4 15 and 7. 26].

## WSC 23

*Q. 23. What Offices doth Christ execute as our Redeemer?*

A. Christ as our Redeemer executeth the offices of a Prophet, of a Priest, and of a King, both in his estate of Humiliation and Exaltation [marg.: Act 3 21 22. Heb. 12 25. with 2 Cor. 13. 3. Heb. 5, 5, 6, 7. and 7. 25. Ps. 2. 6. Isa, 9. 6, 7. Mat. 21. 5. Psa. 2. 8, 9, 10, 11.].

## WSC 24

*Q. 24. How doth Christ execute the Office of a Prophet?*

A. Christ executeth the Office of a Prophet, in revealing to us, by his Word and Spirit, the Will of God for our Salvation [marg.: Ioh. 1. 18 1 Pet. 1. 10 11, 12. Ioh 15. 15. and 29. 31].

## WSC 25

*Q. 25. How doth Christ execute the Office of a Priest?*

A. Christ executeth the Office of a Priest, in his once offering up of himself a sacrifice to satisfie divine justice [marg.: Heb. 9 14, 28], and reconcile us to God [marg.: Heb. 2. 17]; and in making continual intercession for us [marg.: Heb. 7. 24 25].

## WSC 26

*Q. 26. How doth Christ execute the Office of a King?*

A. Christ executeth the Office of a King, in subduing us to himself [marg.: Acts 15 14, 15, 16], in ruling [marg.: Is. 33. 22] and defending us [marg.: Is. 32 1, 2], and in restraining and conquering all his and our enemies [marg.: 1 Cor. 15 25. Ps. 110 throughout.].

## WSC 27

*Q. 27. Wherein did Christs Humiliation consist?*

A. Christs Humiliation consisted in his being born, and that in a low condition [marg.: Luk. 27], made under the Law [marg.: Gal. 4. 4], undergoing the miseries of this life [marg.: Heb. 12 2, 3, Isa. 53 2, 3], the wrath of God [marg.: Luke 22 44. Mat. 27. 46], and the cursed death of the Cross [marg.: Phil. 2. 8], in being buried [marg.: 1 Cor. 15 3], & continuing under the power of death for a time [marg.: Act. 2. 24 25, 26, 27 31].

## WSC 28

*Q. 28. Wherein consisteth Christs Exaltation?*

A. Christs Exaltation consisteth in his rising again from the dead on the third day [marg.: 1 Cor. 15 4], in ascending up into Heaven [marg.: Mark 16 19], in sitting at the right hand of God the Father [marg.: Eph. i. 20], and in coming to judg the world at the last day [marg.: Act. 1. 11 and 17. 31].

## WSC 29

*Q. 29. How are we made partakers of the Redemption purchased by Christ?*

A. We are made partakers of the Redemption purchased by Christ, by the effectual Application of it to us [marg.: Ioh. 1. 11 12] by his holy Spirit [marg.: Tit. 3. 5, 6].

## WSC 30

*Q. 30. How doth the Spirit apply to us the Redemption purchased by Christ?*

A. The Spirit applyeth to us the Redemption purchased by Christ, by working Faith in us [marg.: Eph. 1. 13 14. Ioh. 6 37, 39 Eph. 28], and thereby uniting us to Christ in our effectual Calling [marg.: Eph. 3. 17 1 Cor. 1. 9].

## WSC 31

*Q. 31. What is effectual Calling?*

A. Effectual Calling is the work of Gods Spirit [marg.: 2 Tim. 1 9. 2 Thes. 2. 13, 14], whereby, convincing us of our sin and misery [marg.: Act. 2 37], inlightening our minds in the knowledg of Christ [marg.: Acts 26 18.], and renewing our wills [marg.: Ezek. 36. 26, 27.], he doth perswade and inable us to imbrace Jesus Christ freely offered to us in the Gospel [marg.: Ioh. 6. 44, 45. Phil. 2. 13.].

## WSC 32

*Q. 32. What benefits do they that are •ffectually called, partake of in this life?*

A. They that are effectually Called, do in this life partake of Justification [marg.: Ro. 8 •0], Adoption [marg.: Eph. 1. 5], Sanctification, and the several benefits which in this life do either accompany or flow from them [marg.: 1 Cor. 1 26, 30].

## WSC 33

*Q. 33. What is Justification?*

A. Justification is an act of Gods free grace, wherein he pardoneth all our sins [marg.: Ro. 3. 24 25. and 4 6, 7, 8], and accepteth us as righteous in his fight [marg.: 2 Cor. 5 19, 21], only for the Righteousness of Christ imputed to us [marg.: Rom. 5. 17, 18, 19], and received by Faith alone [marg.: Gal. 2. 16 Phil. 3. 9].

## WSC 34

*Q. 34. What is Adoption?*

A. Adoption is an act of Gods free grace [marg.: 1 Io. 3. 1], whereby we are received into the number, and have a right to all the priviledges of the Sons of God [marg.: Ioh. 1. 12 Rom. 8. 17].

## WSC 35

*Q. 35. VVhat is Sanctification?*

A. Sanctification is the work of Gods free grace [marg.: 2 Thes. 2 13], whereby we are renewed in the whole man after the image of God [marg.: Eph. 4. 23, 24] and are inabled more and more to dye unto sin and live unto righteousness [marg.: Rom, 6 4].

## WSC 36

*Q. 36. VVhat are the benefits which in this life do accompany or flow from Justification, Adoption, and Sanctification?*

A. The benefits which in this life do accompany or flow from Justification, Adoption, and Sanctification, are, assurance of Gods love, peace of Conscience [marg.: Rom. 5 1, 2, 5], joy in the holy Ghost [marg.: Rom. 14 17], increase of grace [marg.: Pro 4 18], and perseverance therein to the end [marg.: 1 Io. 5. 13 1 Pet. 1. 5].

## WSC 37

*Q. 37. VVhat benefi•s do Beleevers receive from Christ at death?*

A. The souls of Beleevers are at their death made perfect in holiness [marg.: Heb. 12 23], & do immediately pass into glory [marg.: 2 Cor. 5 1, 6, 8 Phil. 1. 23 Luk. 23. 43]; and their bodies, being still united to Christ [marg.: 1 Thes. 4 14], do rest in their graves [marg.: Isa. 57. 2] till the Resurrection [marg.: Iob 19. 26, 27].

## WSC 38

*Q. 38. VVhat benefits do Beleevers receive from Christ at the Resurrection?*

A. At the Resurrection, Beleevers being raised up in glory [marg.: i Cor, 15 43], shall be openly acknowledged and acquitted in the day of Judgment [marg.: Mat. 25 23. & 10. 32], and made perfectly blessed in full injoying of God [marg.: 1 Joh. 3. 2 1 Cor. 13. 2] to all eternity [marg.: 1 Thes. 4▪ 17, 18].

## WSC 39

*Q. 39. VVhat is the duty which God requireth of man?*

A. The Duty which God requireth of man, is obedience to his revealed will [marg.: Mic. 6. 8 1 Sam. 15 22].

## WSC 40

*Q. 40. What did God at first reveal to man for the rule of his Obedience?*

A. The rule which God at first revealed to man for his Obedience, was, the Moral Law [marg.: Rom. 2, 14, 15. and 10. 5].

## WSC 41

*Q. 41. Where is the Moral Law summarily comprehended?*

A. The Moral Law is summarily comprehended in the Ten Commandments [marg.: Deu. 10. 4].

## WSC 42

*Q. 42. What is the sum of the Ten Commandments?*

A. The sum of the Ten Commandments, is, to love the Lord our God, with all our heart, with all our soul, with all our strength, and with all our mind; and our Neighbor as our selves [marg.: Mat. 22 37, 38, 39, 40]

## WSC 43

*Q. 43. VVhat is the Preface to the Ten Commandments?*

A. The Preface to the Ten Commandments is in these words [I am the Lord thy God, which have brought thee out of the Land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage [marg.: Exod. 20].

## WSC 44

*Q. 44. What doth the Preface to the Ten Commandments tea•h us?*

A. The Preface to the Ten Commandments teacheth us, that because God is the Lord, and our God, and Redeemer, therefore we are bound to keep all his Commandments [marg.: Luk. 1 74 75. 1 Pet. 1 15, 16, 17, 18, 19].

## WSC 45

*Q. 45. Which is the first Commandment?*

A. The first Commandment is (Thou shalt h•ve no other Gods before me [marg.: Exod. 20 3].)

## WSC 46

*Q. 46. VVhat is required in the first Commandment?*

A. The first Commandment requireth us to know and acknowledg God to be the only true God, and our God [marg.: 1 Chro. 28. 9. Deut 26. 17]; and to worship and glorifie him accordingly [marg.: Mat 4 10 Psa. 29. 2].

## WSC 47

*Q. 47. What is forbidden in the first Commandment?*

A. The first Commandment forbiddeth the denying [marg.: Psa. 14. 1], or not worshiping and glorifying the true God, as God [marg.: Ro. 1, 21], and our God [marg.: Psa. 81 10, 11]; and the giving that worship and glory to any other, which is due to him alone [marg.: Rom. 1. 25, 26].

## WSC 48

*Q. 48. What are we specially taught by these words (Before me) in the first Commandment?*

A. These words (Before me) in the first Commandment, teach us, that God, who seeth all things, taketh notice of, and is much displeased with the sin of having any other God [marg.: Ezek. 8. 5 to the end].

## WSC 49

*Q. 49. Which is the second Commandment?*

A. The second Commandment is, Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth; thou shalt not bow down thy self to them, nor serve them; for I the Lord thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the Fathers upon the children, unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me, and shewing mercy unto thousands of them that love me and keep my Commandments [marg.: Exod. 20 4, 5, 6].

## WSC 50

*Q. 50. What is required in the second Commandment?*

A. The second Commandment requireth, the receiving, observing, and keeping pure and intire all such religious Worship and Ordinances, as God hath appointed in his Word [marg.: Deut, 32 46. Mat. 28. 20 Act. 2. 42.].

## WSC 51

*Q. 51. What is forbidden in the second Commandment?*

A. The second Commandment forbiddeth the worshiping of God by Images [marg.: Deut. 4. 15, 16, 17, 18, 19 Exod. 32. 5 8], or any other way not appointed in his Word [marg.: Deut. 12 31, 32].

## WSC 52

*Q. 52. What are the Reasons annexed to the second Commandment?*

A. The Reasons annexed to the second Commandment are, Gods Soveraignty over us [marg.: Psa 95. 2 3, 6], his propriety in us [marg.: Ps. 45. 11], and the zeal he hath to his own Worship [marg.: Exod 34 13, 14].

## WSC 53

*Q. 53. Which is the third Commandment?*

A. The third Commandment is, Thou shalt not take the Name of the Lord thy God in vain, for the Lord will not hold him guiltless that taketh his Name in vain [marg.: Ex. 20. 7].

## WSC 54

*Q. 54. What is required in the third Commandment?*

A. The third Commandment requireth, the holy and reverent use of Gods Names [marg.: Mat. 6. 9 Deu. 28. 58], Titles [marg.: Ps: 68. 4], Attributes [marg.: Rev. 15 3, 4], Ordinances [marg.: Mal. 1 11 14], Words [marg.: Psa. 138 1, 2], and Works [marg.: Iob 36 24].

## WSC 55

*Q. 55. What is forbidden in the third Commandment?*

A. The third Commandment forbideth all profaning or abusing of any thing whereby God makes himself known [marg.: Mal. 1. 6 •, 12. & 2 •. & 3. 14].

## WSC 56

*Q. 56. What is the Reason annexed to the third Commandment?*

A. The Reason annexed to the third Commandment is, that however the breakers of this Commandment may escape punishment from men, yet the Lord our God will not suffer them to escape his righteous judgment [marg.: 1 Sam. 2 12, 17, 22 29. 1 Sam 3. 13. Deut 28. 58, 59].

## WSC 57

*Q. 57. Which is the fourth Commandment?*

A. The fourth Commandment is, Remember the Sabbath day to keep it holy, Six days shall thou labour and do all thy work, but the seventh day is the Sabbath of the Lord thy God, in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy Man-servant, nor thy Maid servant, nor thy cattel, nor the stranger that is within thy gates; For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, the sea and all that in them is, and rested the seventh day, wherefore the Lord blessed the Sabbath day, and hallowed it [marg.: Exod 20 8, 9, 10, 11].

## WSC 58

*Q. 58. VVhat is required in the fourth Commandment?*

A. The fourth Commandment requireth the keeping holy to God such set time as he hath appointed in his Word, expresly one whole day in seven to be a holy Sabbath to himself [marg.: Deut. 15 12, 13, 14].

## WSC 59

*Q. 59. Which day of the seven hath GOD appointed to be the weekly Sabbath?*

A. From the beginning of the World to the Resurrection of Christ, God appointed the seventh day of the week to be the weekly Sabbath; and the first day of the week ever since, to continue to the end of the world, which is the Christian Sabbath [marg.: Gen. 2. 2 3. 1 Cor. 16. 1, 2 Acts 20. 7].

## WSC 60

*Q. 60. How is the Sabbath to be sanctified?*

A. The Sabbath is to be sanctified by a holy resting all that day [marg.: Exod. 20 8, 10], even from such worldly imployments and recreations as are lawful on other days [marg.: Exod. 16 25, 26, 27, 26 Neh. 13. 5 16, 17, 18, 19, 21 22.], and spending the whole time in the publ•ke and private exercises of Gods worship [marg.: Luk. 4 16 Acts 20, 7 Ps 92. title Isa. 66. 23], except so much as is to be taken up in the works of necessity & mercy [marg.: Mat. 12. 1, to 13].

## WSC 61

*Q. 61. What is forbidden in the fourth Commandment?*

A. The fourth Commandment forbiddeth the omission or careless performance of the duties required [marg.: Ezek. 22 25 Amos 8. 5 Mal. 1. 13], and the prophaning the day by idleness [marg.: Acts 20 7, 9.], or doing that which is in it self sinful [marg.: Ezek. 23 38], or by unnecessary thoughts, words, or works about worldly imployments or recreations [marg.: Jer. 17. 24 25, 26 Isai. 58, 13].

## WSC 62

*Q. 62. What are the Reasons annexed to the fourth Commandment?*

A. The Reasons annexed to the fourth Commandment are, Gods allowing us six days of the week for our own imployments [marg.: Exo. 20, 9], his challenging a special propriety in the seventh, his own example, and his blessing the Sabbath day [marg.: Exod. 20 11].

## WSC 63

*Q. 63. Which is the fifth Commandment?*

A. The fifth Commandment is, Honor thy father and thy mother, that thy days may be long upon the Land which the Lord thy God giveth thee [marg.: Exod. 20 12].

## WSC 64

*Q. 64. VVhat is required in the fifth Commandment?*

A. The fifth Commandment requireth the preserving the honour, and performing the duties, belonging to every one in their several places & relations, as Superiors [marg.: Eph. 5. 21], Inferiors [marg.: Pet. 2 17], or Equals [marg.: Ro. 12. 10].

## WSC 65

*Q. 65. VVhat is forbidden in the fifth Commandment?*

A. The fifth Commandment forbiddeth the neglecting of, or doing any thing against the honor and duty which belongeth to every one in their several places and relations [marg.: Mat. 5, 15 4, 5, 6. Ezek. 34 2 3, 4 Rom. 13, 8].

## WSC 66

*Q. 66. What is the Reason annexed to the fifth Commandment?*

A. The Reason annexed to the fifth Commandment is a promise of long life and prosperity (as far as it shal serve for Gods glory, and their own good) to all such as keep this Commandment [marg.: Deu. 5, 16 Eph 6, 2, 3].

## WSC 67

*Q. 67. Which is the sixth Commandment?*

A. The sixth Commandment is (Thou shall not kill [marg.: Exod. 20 13].)

## WSC 68

*Q. 68. What is required in the 6 Comandment*

A. The sixth Commandment requireth all lawful endeavors to prese•ve our own life [marg.: Eph, 5, 28, 29,], and the life of others [marg.: 1 Kings 18, 4,].

## WSC 69

*Q. 69. What is forbidden in the sixth Commandment?*

A. The sixth Commandment forbiddeth the t•k•ng away of our own life, or the life of our neighbor unjustly; and whatsoever tendeth thereunto [marg.: Acts 16, 28, Gen, 9 6.].

## WSC 70

*Q. 70. Which is the seventh Commandment?*

A. The seventh Commandment is, Thou shalt not commit adultery [marg.: Exod. 20 14].

## WSC 71

*Q. 71. What is required in the seventh Commandment?*

A. The seventh Commandment req•ireth the preservation of our own and our neighbors chastity, in heart, speech, and behaviour [marg.: 1 Cor. 7 2, 3. 5 34, 36 Col 4 6. 1 Pet. 3 2].

## WSC 72

*Q. 72. What is forbidden in the seventh Commandment?*

A. The seventh Commandment forbiddeth all unchast thoughts, words and actions [marg.: Mat. 15 19, & 5, 28 Eph. 5 3, 4].

## WSC 73

*Q. 73. Which is the eighth Commandement?*

A. The eighth Commandement is, [Thou shalt not steal [marg.: Exod. 20 15],]

## WSC 74

*Q. 74. What is required in the eight Commandement?*

A. The eighth Commandement requireth the lawfull procuring, and furthering the wealth and outward estate of our selves, and others [marg.: Gen. 30, 30. 1 Tim. 5, 8],

## WSC 75

*Q. 75. What is forbidden in the eighth Commandment?*

A. The eighth Commandment forbiddeth whatsoever doth, or may unjustly hinder our own, or our neighbors wealth, or outward estate [marg.: Lev. 25, 35. Deut. 22, 1 2, 3, 4 5. Exod. 2 3, 4, 5 Gen. 47, 14, 20 Pro, 21, 17 & 23, 20, 21. & 28. 19. Ephe. 4. 28].

## WSC 76

*Q. 76. Which is the ninth Commandment?*

A. The ninth Commandment is, [Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbor [marg.: Exo, 20 16].)

## WSC 77

*Q. 77. What is required in the ninth Commandment?*

A. The ninth Commandment requireth the maintaining and promoting of truth between man and man [marg.: Zach. 8, 16], and of our own and our neighbors good name [marg.: 3 John v. 12] especially in witness bearing [marg.: Prov. 14 5, 25].

## WSC 78

*Q. 78. What is forbidden in the ninth Commandment?*

A. The ninth Commandment forbiddeth whatsoever is prejudicial to truth, or injurious to our own, or our neighbors good name [marg.: 1 Sam, 17, 28 Lev. 19, 16 Psal. 15, 3].

## WSC 79

*Q. 79. Which is the tenth Commandment?*

A. The tenth Commandment is, Thou shalt not covet thy neighbors house, thou shalt not covet thy neighbors wife, nor his man-servant, nor his maid-servant, nor his Ox, nor his Asse, nor any thing that is thy neighbors [marg.: Exod. 20 17].

## WSC 80

*Q. 80. What is required in the tenth Commandment?*

A. The tenth Commandment requireth full contentment with our own condition [marg.: Heb. 13. 5 1 Tim. 6. 6], with a right and charitable frame of spirit toward our neighbor, and all that is his [marg.: Job 31. 29. Rom. 12. 15. 1 Tim. 1, 5. 1 Cor. 13 4, 5, 6, 7.].

## WSC 81

*Q. 81. What is forbidden in the tenth Commandment?*

A. The tenth Commandment forbiddeth all discontentment with our own estate [marg.: 1 Kin. 21 4. Esther 5. 13. 1 Cor. 10. 10.], envying or grieving at the good of our neighbor [marg.: Gal. 5 26. Jam. 3. 14. 16.], and all inordinate motions and affections to any thing that is his [marg.: Rom. 7. 7, 8. & 13. 9. Deut. 5 21.].

## WSC 82

*Q. 82. Is any man able perfectly to keep the Commandments of God?*

A. No meer man since the fall, is able in this life, perfectly to keep the Commandments of God [marg.: Eccl. 7. 20. 1 Joh. 1. 8. 10. Gal. 5. 17], but doth dayly break them in thought, word, & deed [marg.: Gen. 6. 5. & 8. 21 Rom. 3, 9. to 21. •am 3. 2. to 13].

## WSC 83

*Q. 83. Are all transgressio•s of the Law equally heinous?*

A. Some sins in themselves, and by reason of several aggravations are more heinous in the sight of God then others [marg.: Ez•k 8 6, 13. 15. 1 Joh. 5. 16. Psa. 78 17, 32▪ 56.]

## WSC 84

*Q. 84. What doth every sin deserve?*

A. Every sin deserveth Gods wrath, and curse, both in this life, and that which is to come [marg.: Eph, •. 6 Gal. 3. 10 Iam. 3 39 Mat. 25. 41].

## WSC 85

*Q. 85. What doth God require of us, that we may escape his wrath and curse, due to us for sin?*

A. To escape the wrath and curse of God due to us for sin, God requireth of us Faith in Jesus Christ, Repentance unto life [marg.: Act. 20. 21], with the diligent use of all the outward means, wherby Christ communicateth to us the benefits of Redemption [marg.: Pro. 2. 1, to 6. & 8. 33. to the end. Isal. 59. 3].

## WSC 86

*Q. 86. What is Faith in Jesus Christ?*

A. Faith in Jesus Christ is a saving grace [marg.: Heb, 10, 39], whereby we receive, and rest upon him alone for Salvation, as he is offered to us in the Gospel [marg.: Joh. 1. 12 Isa. 26. 3, 4 Phil. 3. 9 Gal. 2. 16].

## WSC 87

*Q. 87. What is repentance unto life?*

A. Repentance unto life is a saving grace [marg.: Act. 11, 18], whereby a sinner out of a true sense of his sin [marg.: Acts 2, 37, 38], and apprehension of the mercy of God in Christ [marg.: Joel 2. 12 Jer. 3. 22], doth, with grief and hatred of his sin, turn from it unto God [marg.: Jer. 31, 18, 19 Eze. 36, 31], with full purpose of, and endeavor after new obedience [marg.: 1 Cor. 7 11. Isa. 1, 16, 17].

## WSC 88

*Q. 88. What are the outward means whereby Christ communicateth to us the benefits of Redemption?*

A. The outward and ordinary means, whereby Christ communicateth to us the benefits of Redemption, are, his Ordinances, especially the Word, Sacraments, and Prayer; all which are made effectual to the Elect, for Salvation [marg.: Mat. 28 19, 20 Acts 2. 42 46, 47].

## WSC 89

*Q. 89. How is the Word made effectual to Salvation?*

A. The Spirit of God maketh the Reading, but especially the Preaching of the Word, an effectual means of convincing and converting sinners, and of building them up in holiness and comfort through faith unto salvation [marg.: Neh 8 8 1 Cor. 14, 24, 25 Act. 26. 18 Psal. 19 8 Act. 20 32 Rom. 15 4 2 Tim. 3. 15, 16, 17. Ro. 10. 13 14, 15, 16, 17. & 1 16].

## WSC 90

*Q. 90. How is the Word to be read and heard, that it may become effectual to salvation?*

A. That the Word may become effectual to salvation, we must attend thereunto with diligence [marg.: Prov. 8. 34.], preparation [marg.: 1 Pet. 2 1, 2.], and prayer [marg.: Psa. 119. 18.], receive it with faith and love [marg.: Heb. 4 2 2 Thes. 2. 10.], lay it up in our hearts [marg.: Psa. 119 11.], and practise it in our lives [marg.: Luk. 8. 15. Jam. 1. 25].

## WSC 91

*Q. 91. How do the Sacraments become effectual means of salvation?*

A. The Sacraments become effectual means of salvation, not from any vertue in them, or in him that doth administer them, but only by the blessing of Christ [marg.: 1 Pet. 3. 21. Mat 3. 11. 1 Cor. 3. 6, 7], and the working of his Spirit, in them that by faith receive them [marg.: 1 Cor. 12, 23].

## WSC 92

*Q. 92. What is a Sacrament;*

A. A Sacrament is an holy Ordinance instituted by Christ, wherein, by sensible Signs, Christ and the benefits of the New Covenant are represented, sealed and applyed to Beleevers [marg.: Gen. 17 7, 10 Exod. 12. 1 Cor. 11 23, 26].

## WSC 93

*Q. 93. Which are the Sacraments of the New Testament?*

A. The Sacraments of the New Testament are, Baptism [marg.: Mat. 28 19], and the Lords Supper [marg.: Mat. 26 26, 27, 28].

## WSC 94

*Q. 94. VVhat is Baptism?*

A. Baptism is a Sacrament, wherein the Washing with Water, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost [marg.: Mat. 28 19], doth signifie and seal our ingrafting into Christ, and partaking of the benefits of the Covenant of Grace, and our engagement to be the Lords [marg.: Ro 6, 24 Gal. 2, 27].

## WSC 95

*Q. 95. To whom is Baptism to be administred?*

A. Baptism is not to be administred to any that are out of the Visible Church, till they profess their faith in Christ, and obedience to him [marg.: Act. 8, 36 37. and 2, 38], but the infants of such as are Members of the Visible Church are to be baptized [marg.: Acts 2 38, 39 Gen. 17 10. with Col. 2, 11 12. 1 Cor. 7, 14].

## WSC 96

*Q. 96. VVhat is the Lords Supper?*

A. The Lords Supper is a Sacrament, wherein, by giving and receiving Bread and Wine according to Christs appointment, his death is shewed forth; and the worthy receivers are not after a corporal and carnal manner, but by faith made partakers of his Body and Blood, with all his benefits to their spiritual nourishment and growth in grace [marg.: 1 Cor. 11 23, 24, 25, 26. & 10, 16].

## WSC 97

*Q. 97. What is required to the worthy receiving of the Lords Supper?*

A. It is required of them that would worthily partake of the Lords Supper, that they examine themselves, of their Knowledg to discern the Lords Body [marg.: 1 Cor. 11 28, 29], of their Faith to feed upon him [marg.: 2 Cor. 13 5], of their Repentance [marg.: 1 Cor. 11 31], love [marg.: 1 Cor, 10 16, 17], and new obedience [marg.: 1 Cor 5, 7 8]; lest, coming unworthily, they eat and drink judgment to themselves [marg.: 1 Cor. 11 28. 29].

## WSC 98

*Q. 98. What is Prayer?*

A. Prayer is an offering up of our desires to God [marg.: Psa. 62. 8], for things agreeable to his will [marg.: 1 Ioh, 5 14], in the Name of Christ [marg.: Ioh. 16, 23], with confession of our sins [marg.: Psal, 32, 5, 6 Dan. 9 4], and thankful acknowledgment of his mercies [marg.: Phil, 4, 6].

## WSC 99

*Q. 99. What rule hath God given for our direction in Prayer?*

A. The whole Word of God is of use to direct us in Prayer [marg.: 1 Ioh, 5 14], but the special rule of direction, is, that form of Prayer which Christ taught his Disciples, commonly called, The Lords Prayer [marg.: Math. 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 with Luke 11, 2 3, 4].

## WSC 100

*Q. 100. What doth the Preface of the Lords Prayer teach us?*

A. The Preface of the Lords Prayer, which is, (Our Father which art in Heaven [marg.: Mat, 6, 9],) teacheth us to draw neer to God, with all holy reverence and confidence, as children to a father, able and ready to help us [marg.: Ro. 8, 15 Luk, 11, 13]; and that we should pray with and for others [marg.: Act 12, 5 1 Tim. 2. 1. 2].

## WSC 101

*Q. 101. What do we pray for in the first Petition?*

A. In the first Petition, which is, (Hallowed be thy name [marg.: Mat, 6, 9]) we pray, that God would enable us and others to glorifie him in all that whereby he maketh himself known [marg.: Psal, 67, 2, 3], and that he would dispose all things to his own glory [marg.: Psa, 83, throughout.].

## WSC 102

*Q. 102. What do we pray for in the second Petition?*

A. In the second Petition, which is, (Thy Kingdom come [marg.: Mat, 6. 10],) we pray that Satans Kingdom may be destroyed [marg.: Psa, 68 1 18], and that the Kingdom of Grace may be advanced [marg.: Rev, 12, 10, 11], our selves and others brought into it, and kept in it [marg.: 2 Thess, 3, 1. Rom, 10, 1, Ioh. 17, 9, 20], and that the Kingdom of Glory may be hastened [marg.: Rev, 22 20].

## WSC 103

*Q. 103. What do we pray for in the third Petition?*

A. In the third Petition, which is, (Thy will be done on earth, as it is in heaven [marg.: Mat. 6 10],) we pray, that God by his grace would make us able and willing to know, obey, and submit to his will in all things [marg.: Psa. 67, throughout. Ps. 119, 36 mat. 26 39 2 Sam. 15 25. Iob 1 21], as the Angels do in heaven [marg.: Psa. 103. 20, 21].

## WSC 104

*Q. 104. What do we pray for in the fourth Petition?*

A. In the fourth Petition, which is, (Give us this day our dayly bread [marg.: Mat. 6. 11],) we pray, that of Gods free gift we may receive a competent portion of the good things of this life, and enjoy his blessing with them [marg.: Prov. 30 8, 9 Gen. 28, 20. 1 Tim, 4. 4, 5].

## WSC 105

*Q. 105. What do we pray for in the fifth Petition?*

A. In the fisth Petition, which is, (And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debters [marg.: Mat. 6. 12],) we pray, that God for Christs sake would freely pardon all our sins [marg.: Psa. 51. 1, 2, 7, 9 Dan. 9. 17, 18, 19]; which we are the rather encouraged to ask, because by his grace we are enabled from the heart to forgive others [marg.: Luk. 11. 4 Mat 18. 15]

## WSC 106

*Q. 106. What do we pray for in the sixth Petition?*

A. In the sixth Petition, which is, (And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil [marg.: Mat. 6. 13],) we pray, that God would either keep us from being tempted to sin [marg.: Mat 26 41], or support and deliver us when we are tempted [marg.: 2 Cor 12. 1, 8].

## WSC 107

*Q. 107. What doth the Conclusion of the Lords Prayer teach us?*

A. The Conclusion of the Lords Prayer, which is, (For thine is the Kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen [marg.: Mat. 6. 13].) teacheth us to take our encouragement in prayer from God onely [marg.: Dan. 9. 4, 7, 8, 9, 16, 17, 18, 19.], and in our Prayers to praise him, ascribing Kingdom, power, and glory to him [marg.: 1 Chro. 29. 10, 11, 12, 13]; and in testimony of our desire and assurance to be heard, we say, Amen [marg.: 1 Cor. 14 16. Rev. 22. 20, 21].
